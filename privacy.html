Privacy Policy

Private YouTube Video Transfer

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Purpose

This application is being developed for personal video backup. Its intended purpose is to transfer selected videos from the application owner's Microsoft OneDrive account to the owner's YouTube channel with Private visibility.

2. Information Accessed

When authorized, the application may access selected OneDrive video files, filenames, and related metadata. It requests the YouTube upload permission to upload videos to the authorized YouTube channel.

3. Use of Information

Information is used to perform authorized video transfers, identify completed uploads, and avoid duplicate transfers.

4. Storage and Security

The planned automation will use a secured cloud server to process transfers. Videos may be stored temporarily during uploading. Authentication credentials will not be published on this website or in its public source repository.

5. Sharing

Uploaded videos are intended to be marked Private on YouTube. Video data is transferred to Microsoft and Google services as needed for the authorized backup process. The application is not intended to sell personal information or share videos with advertisers.

6. Data Retention

The planned system will retain operational records needed to identify completed uploads. Temporary video copies will be removed after successful processing, subject to recovery and error-handling requirements. The original OneDrive files will not be deleted automatically.

7. Revoking Access

The owner can revoke Google access from Google Account security settings and Microsoft access from Microsoft account application permissions.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as the application is developed or its functionality changes.
